Method

01 / FALSIFIABLE

Measure the oracle first.

Before optimizing a predictor or implementation, establish whether the best possible decision has enough headroom to matter.

02 / EVIDENCE

Keep the receipts.

Claims link outward to code, raw results and plots. The public write-up is allowed to be elegant because the underlying artifacts remain inspectable.

03 / NEGATIVE RESULTS

Kill branches in public.

A failed transistor topology or an absent GPU oracle gap is a research result. Rejected branches stay visible instead of being rewritten as progress.
